# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 5, 2026**

Teen Effort LLC ("Teen Effort," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Teen Effort mobile application and website (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It is designed to be consistent with the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), and other applicable privacy laws.

Teen Effort is for users ages 13 and older.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Give Us

- **Account Information.** Name, email address, username, password, and date of birth. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email address that provider shares with us. Your date of birth is collected once at signup and cannot be changed afterward.
- **Photo Journal Content.** Photos and media you upload, including profile pictures.
- **Love Letters.** Written messages you exchange with your linked partner.
- **Vision Board Content.** Images, links, text, and goals you add.
- **Compatibility Quiz Responses.** Your answers. Results are calculated on your device and are not processed by AI or any third party.
- **Wishlists, Gift Ideas, and Reviews.** Content you add to these features.
- **Saved Dates and Venues.** Calendar entries and the addresses of venues you choose to save. A saved venue's address is the venue's location, not yours.
- **Home City and Zip Code.** A home city you type is stored on your profile and is visible to your linked partner (it powers the Distance feature). A zip code is also stored on your profile and is used for recommendations when Precise Location is turned off. Your partner does not see your zip code.
- **AI Prompt Text.** The filters and text you enter when you ask for date or gift ideas (for example budget, setting, activity, food type, and any details you choose to type).
- **Reports and Safety Messages.** Reports you submit, including the reason and a copy of the reported content, and messages you send to our support email.
- **Payment Information.** Payments are processed by Stripe on the web and by Apple or Google, through RevenueCat, in the mobile apps. We do not store your full payment card number.
- **Partner Linking Data.** Invite codes and link or unlink status between two accounts. Linking is restricted by age band: accounts ages 13–17 can link only with other accounts ages 13–17, and accounts ages 18 and older can link only with other accounts ages 18 and older.
- **Unlink Summary (Optional).** If both partners agree when unlinking, a PDF summary of the relationship (for example, the number of dates and Photo Journal highlights) can be created. Each partner's agreement is recorded on the partner link. If either partner does not agree, no summary is created.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **On-Demand Location.** When you use a feature that needs your location (date ideas, map view, or weather), we ask your device for its location. Your phone holds that location in its short-term memory for up to five minutes to avoid repeated requests; it is not saved on your phone. Our servers do not store or log your coordinates or city name in their logs. Your device location is never shown to your linked partner or any other user. See Section 6 for how location is sent to the services that power these features.
- **Technical Data.** Our hosting and database providers process standard technical data, such as IP address, timestamps, and error messages, to deliver the App and keep it secure.

We do not use analytics tools, advertising tools, tracking technologies, or crash-reporting tools in the App.

### 1.3 Information From Third Parties

- Purchase and subscription status from RevenueCat (an anonymous user identifier and purchase data only) and transaction confirmations from Stripe.
- Name and email address from Apple or Google, if you use them to sign in.
- AI-generated date and gift suggestions from Lovable's AI Gateway, which uses Google Gemini. See Section 2.

## 2. How We Use Personal Information

- **Provide the App.** Run the Photo Journal, Love Letters, Vision Board, saved dates, Compatibility Quiz, Distance feature, and other features.
- **Enable Partner Linking.** Share content between two linked accounts using an invite code.
- **Process Payments.** Manage subscriptions through Stripe, Apple, Google, and RevenueCat.
- **Generate Suggestions.** Create date and gift ideas using a third-party AI model. The AI receives only the text and filters you enter, plus your city name and the names and distances of nearby venues. It never receives your coordinates, your name, your partner's information, your saved dates, your wishlists, or any of your photos, letters, or journal entries. Please do not type personal details about yourself or other people into AI prompts. We do not use your prompts to train AI models ourselves. How Lovable and Google handle prompts is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.
- **Communicate With You.** Send account, security, and service emails.
- **Keep Users Safe.** Review reports, enforce our rules, detect fraud, and prevent misuse. Content you report (up to 2,000 characters of it, including Love Letter text) is sent to our team for review. No one on our team reads private content otherwise, and we do not use AI to scan or moderate photos, letters, or journal content.
- **Fix Problems.** Respond to issues you report to us.
- **Meet Legal Obligations.** Respond to valid legal requests and protect users' safety.

We do not use personal information for targeted advertising, and we do not sell personal information.

## 3. How We Disclose Personal Information

- **Your Linked Partner.** Content you create, including photos, letters, Vision Board items, saved dates, home city, and quiz results, is visible to your linked partner while you are linked. If you turn off sharing for saved date ideas or wishlists in Settings, they are not shared. When you unlink, your partner's access ends on our servers right away. Anything your partner already downloaded or screenshots stays on their device, and we cannot recall it.
- **Service Providers.** Companies that help us run the App, listed in Section 10. They may use the information only to provide services to us.
- **Safety and Legal Reasons.** Law enforcement or regulators when required by law or valid legal process, or when needed to protect someone's safety.
- **Business Transfers.** In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing the personal information of anyone under 16.

The App does not make your name or content visible to anyone other than your linked partner (and our team, for reports). If we ever add a feature that makes content visible to other users, we will update this policy and notify you in the App before that feature launches.

### What Deleting Your Account Does

Deleting your account removes your profile and content from our database, including your journal entries, letters, wishlists, gift ideas, reviews, quiz answers, saved dates, and partner links. It also deletes your uploaded photo and profile picture files from storage. Some things may remain after deletion:

- Copies in routine backups kept by our hosting provider, until those backups are overwritten in the ordinary course.
- Standard server logs, which do not contain your coordinates or city name.
- Reports filed by or about you, and records of safety actions, which we keep for safety and legal purposes.
- Any PDF your partner already saved to their own device, which we cannot delete.

There is no account "pause" option. Deleting your account is permanent.

## 5. Minors' Privacy

Teen Effort is intended for users ages 13 and older. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 13 to create an account. We ask for your date of birth at signup and decline to create an account if it shows you are under 13. After a declined attempt we apply a waiting period before another attempt. Your date of birth cannot be changed after it is set.

To reduce the risk of inappropriate contact between minors and adults, partner linking is limited by age band: accounts ages 13–17 can link only with other accounts ages 13–17, and accounts ages 18 and older can link only with other accounts ages 18 and older. We enforce this on our servers. This does not remove every age-related risk inside the 13–17 band, for example a 13-year-old linking with a 17-year-old, so please link only with someone you know and trust.

For users under 18, Precise Location is off by default. Right after a teen creates an account, we ask once whether they want to turn it on, and they can change it at any time in Settings. We do not use personal information from any user for advertising, and we do not use AI to profile users.

A parent or guardian who believes a child has given us personal information in a way that conflicts with this policy can email [support.teeneffort@gmail.com](mailto:support.teeneffort@gmail.com) to ask us to review or delete it. We may ask for details to confirm the request is genuine before acting on it.

## 6. Location Data

Teen Effort asks for your device location only when you use a feature that needs it: date suggestions, map view, or weather. You can allow or deny this in your device settings at any time, and denying it may limit those features. In Settings, the Precise Location switch controls how exact the location is that we use.

- **If Precise Location is on**, your phone sends its exact coordinates to the services below.
- **If Precise Location is off**, your phone rounds coordinates to about 1 kilometer before they leave your device, and we use your zip code for recommendations.

Services that receive location:

- **Open-Meteo** (weather): coordinates.
- **OpenStreetMap Nominatim** (finding your city name): coordinates.
- **OpenStreetMap Overpass** (finding nearby venues): coordinates and a search radius.
- **Google Gemini through Lovable's AI Gateway** (AI suggestions): your city name and the names and distances of nearby venues only, never coordinates.

Your phone holds your location in short-term memory for up to five minutes and does not save it. Our servers do not write your coordinates or city name to their logs.

**Shared calendar entries.** When you add a date idea to the shared calendar, the search location used to find that idea is saved with that entry. If Precise Location is on, this location can be precise; if it is off, it is rounded to about 1 kilometer. Because the calendar is shared, your linked partner can see the entry. Venue addresses you save are the venue's location, not yours.

Maps links in the App contain only a venue's address.

## 7. Reporting and Safety

Teen Effort has zero tolerance for harassment, explicit content, and abuse.

- **Report.** You can report a letter, review, or request using the flag in the App. A report goes to our team's review queue with the reason, the type of content, and a copy of up to 2,000 characters of the content. Only our team can see reports.
- **Block.** Blocking someone unlinks your accounts and permanently prevents the two of you from linking again.
- **Response.** We review reports as quickly as we can and may remove content or accounts that break our rules. We do not use automated scanning of private content, so reports are how we find problems.
- **Urgent concerns.** Email [support.teeneffort@gmail.com](mailto:support.teeneffort@gmail.com). If someone is in danger, call 911.

## 8. Data Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your data as described in Section 4, with the exceptions listed there. We do not keep a history of your device location.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
